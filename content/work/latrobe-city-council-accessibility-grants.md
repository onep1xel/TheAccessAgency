    It's really encouraging to see local government backing accessible tourism with real funding.

    The Access Agency worked with [Latrobe City Council](https://www.latrobe.vic.gov.au/ "Latrobe City Council website") to design and deliver a new grants program helping tourism operators make their businesses more accessible — turning good intentions into upgrades on the ground for the small businesses and not-for-profits who often can't absorb the cost alone.

    **The Access Agency was engaged in a holistic manner:**

    - **Helping determine the grant eligibility criteria**
    - **Assessing grant submissions**
    - **Running an indepth, in-person information session**
    - **Designing promtional material**

    [Accessibility Improvement Grants](https://www.latrobe.vic.gov.au/Accessibility_Improvement_Grant "The Accessibility Improvement Grant webpage")  offers Latrobe City small businesses and not-for-profits funding for accessibility upgrades including tactile signage and wayfinding, step-free access, visitor audio guides, staff training in access and inclusion, and accessible toilet facilities.

    - $25,000 maximum funding — Major round
    - $1,000 maximum funding — Minor round, no co-contribution required
